# Product

This area describes the domain, supported outcomes and deliberate boundaries.
It is useful when deciding what to build, reporting a defect or reviewing behavior.

- [Purpose and scope](scope.md): users, responsibilities and supported capability.
- [Behavior and acceptance](behavior.md): observable guarantees and failure cases.
- [Roadmap and priorities](roadmap.md): current work versus proposals.

Implementation details belong in [Technical](../technical/index.md), and practical
operation belongs in the [User Guide](../user-guide/index.md).
